The Maroon 9 Creative Hub

Building a Home for Creativity, Opportunity & the Future

Maroon 9 has a bold vision: to establish a permanent youth arts and creative workforce facility in Tarrant County that expands access to the arts while connecting young people to education, leadership, entrepreneurship, and creative career opportunities.

The Maroon 9 Creative Hub will be a place where youth can learn, create, rehearse, perform, produce, and explore careers behind and beyond the stage. The facility will provide dedicated space for theatre and arts education, performances, workshops, creative workforce training, mentorship, youth leadership, and hands-on exposure to careers in the arts, entertainment, media, production, and other creative industries.

The Hub will also create opportunities for teaching artists, creative professionals, schools, community organizations, and industry partners to share expertise, collaborate, and connect young people with real-world experiences. Youth will have opportunities to move from their first arts experience to advanced training, mentorship, paid opportunities, and greater awareness of educational and career possibilities.

Why Tarrant County Needs the Hub

More Than a Building

Young people need more than occasional access to the arts—they need consistent spaces where creativity can lead to opportunity. The M9 Hub will help address gaps in access by creating a dedicated, youth-centered environment where arts education, youth development, and creative workforce exposure exist under one roof.

We envision the Maroon 9 Creative Hub as more than a building. It will be a community asset and ecosystem for creative youth development, strengthening connections among young people, families, schools, artists, employers, and community partners while contributing to the cultural and creative growth of Tarrant County.

Help Us Build What's Next

Invest in the Next Generation

Supporting the Hub is an investment in the next generation of artists, creators, leaders, entrepreneurs, and creative professionals. Community support will help Maroon 9 secure and develop the facility, expand accessible opportunities for youth, and build the infrastructure needed to sustain this work for generations to come.
