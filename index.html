Preparing Youth for Life Beyond the Stage

Maroon 9 is a Tarrant County, Texas-based nonprofit organization that inspires, empowers, and develops youth through the transformative power of the arts. Through theatre-based learning, youth development, conflict resolution, creative workforce exposure, and community engagement, we create opportunities for young people to discover their voice, build confidence, develop leadership skills, explore creative careers, and prepare for their future.

Our work extends beyond the stage. We use theatre and creativity as tools to strengthen youth, connect learning to real-world opportunities, and build stronger families, schools, and communities.

What We Do

Youth Development Through the Arts

Maroon 9 provides year-round community theatre programs for youth ages 5–17 throughout Dallas–Fort Worth, often at no cost through partnerships with schools, districts, community centers, and local organizations.

Our signature Kids Acting With Purpose (KAWP) program takes youth beyond theatre training through performances, field trips, mentorship, creative workforce exploration, and paid opportunities—building confidence, leadership, and skills for life beyond the stage.

Explore KAWP
Conflict Resolution Through Theatre™

Our signature theatre-based curriculum equips youth with practical tools to navigate conflict safely and responsibly. Through the Know Your W.O.R.T.H. framework, real-life stories, role-playing, and interactive theatre, participants learn five safe, nonviolent conflict de-escalation strategies while strengthening communication, decision-making, and problem-solving skills.

Learn More
Arts Education Services

Through Page To Stage Arts Education Services, our social enterprise, we provide schools and community organizations with curriculum, professional development, theatre workshops and master classes, plays, and performance booking services. Revenue generated through Page To Stage is reinvested into Maroon 9’s mission, helping expand access to youth arts programming and opportunities throughout the community.

Sickle Cell Advocacy

Maroon9 Sickle Cell Stars® empowers youth living with Sickle Cell Disease through care, creativity, and celebration while increasing community awareness and connecting families to support.
